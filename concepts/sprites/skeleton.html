Gate · base skeleton

Skeleton + base body

Layer 1 of the swappable character: a Mixamo-named armature built from one proportions table, and a neutral angular body whose segments are each bound to a single bone. No hair or clothes yet. Those come later as separate meshes on the same bones. Every sprite here is rendered and pixelized automatically; no pixel was touched by hand.

The gate: this passes when

  1. the bare body reads as a human with the right proportions in all 8 facings at 1×;
  2. every test pose bends the right way (knees back, elbows forward), no limb passes through the body, and the feet stay on the ground;
  3. the bone overlay sits inside the body where you'd expect the joints.
