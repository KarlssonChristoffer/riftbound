Pro model + crease test

Two tests from the 3D pixel-art research, both through the same Blender camera (20° pitch), the same 96×80 canvas and pivot, the same figure height (55 px) and the same palette and outline rules. Our Ranger is the code-built model. KayKit Rogue is a professionally modelled CC0 character (Kay Lousberg), with its own rig and clips. Crease adds 1-px highlights on convex creases, only on the nearer pixel (pixel.js / Kyriota rule).
