The KitagawaGameDev method: Blender renders exact, un-antialiased ID / normal / depth passes through an ortho camera whose pixel grid is the 48×64 canvas, turning the character in 8 steps. pixelize.py then paints them with the master's own rules (these palette families, top-left light in 3 bands, interior lines, local outline, clean-up). Camera locked at 20° pitch. v6 = the round-2 mesh: bigger head with an open face (≈188 face px facing S vs the master's 169), 1-px eyes, arms and bow swung out, scarf under the chin with the tunic edge tucked under, chest strap. v7 = the same model made angular: fewer ring segments, flat shading, saw-toothed fringe, pointed locks and hair clumps, tapered jaw; v7b = 8-sided hair cap. v8 = v7 re-proportioned athletic: ~3.3 heads tall, long legs, V-taper (shoulders 1.1 × , waist 0.72 ×), longer thinner arms, short tunic skirt and mid-thigh cape, longbow. Earlier rounds kept for comparison. Everything here is automatic; no pixel was touched by hand.
